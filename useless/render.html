Help & Instructions

How to Use:

  1. Enter your LaTeX equation or MathML code in the input box
  2. Click "Render Equation" to preview it
  3. Adjust display options if needed (background color, text color, scale, padding)
  4. Click "Copy as Image" to copy the rendered equation to your clipboard
  5. Alternatively, use the download buttons to save as PNG or SVG

LaTeX Syntax Tips:

  • Fractions: \frac{numerator}{denominator}
  • Superscripts: x^2 or x^{a+b} for complex expressions
  • Subscripts: x_i or x_{i+1} for complex expressions
  • Square roots: \sqrt{x} or \sqrt[n]{x} for nth roots
  • Greek letters: \alpha, \beta, \gamma, etc.
  • Integrals: \int_{lower}^{upper} expression \, dx
  • Summations: \sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i

MathML Support:

This tool also supports MathML. Simply paste your MathML code in the input box and render it.
